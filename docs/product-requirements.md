# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Life Operating System

---

## Objective

Create an AI-native operating system capable of managing every domain of an individual's life through autonomous yet transparent decision support.

---

## Primary Users

Initially:

- Single user

Future:

- Families
- Teams
- Organisations

---

## Functional Requirements

The platform shall support:

- Goal management
- Project management
- Task planning
- Knowledge management
- Memory systems
- AI reasoning
- Long-term planning
- Habit tracking
- Calendar integration
- Email integration
- Executive reporting
- Analytics
- Multi-agent collaboration

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Highly modular
- Cloud deployable
- Secure
- Explainable AI
- Extensible
- Event-driven
- Offline-capable where practical
- API-first

---

## Success Criteria

Users should experience measurable improvements in:

- Goal achievement
- Decision quality
- Time utilisation
- Learning consistency
- Health habits
- Research productivity
- Financial planning
