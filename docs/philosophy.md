# Life OS Philosophy

## Core Principle

Life is a dynamic interconnected system.

No decision exists in isolation.

Every action influences future opportunities, risks and constraints.

---

## Optimisation Goal

The purpose of Life OS is not productivity.

The purpose is long-term flourishing.

Every recommendation must improve one or more of the following:

- Health
- Knowledge
- Wealth
- Relationships
- Character
- Purpose
- Freedom
- Legacy

without causing unacceptable degradation elsewhere.

---

## Systems Thinking

Life OS models life as a collection of interacting systems.

Examples include:

- Career
- Research
- Finance
- Health
- Family
- Learning
- Spirituality

Each system influences every other system.

---

## Decision Philosophy

Every recommendation should maximise expected lifetime value.

Trade-offs should be explicit.

Uncertainty should be acknowledged.

Risk should be quantified whenever possible.

---

## Human-Centred AI

The human always retains final authority.

AI advises.

AI analyses.

AI predicts.

AI explains.

Humans decide.
