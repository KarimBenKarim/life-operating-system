# Life OS Glossary

Version: 0.1  
Status: Living Document

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the common vocabulary used throughout the Life OS project.

Every developer, AI agent, architectural document, ADR, API specification, and design discussion should use these terms consistently.

When ambiguity exists, the definitions in this document take precedence.

---

# A

## Action

A concrete piece of work that can be executed.

Examples:

- Send an email
- Read a paper
- Exercise for 30 minutes

Actions are atomic and belong to a Task.

---

## Agent

An autonomous software component responsible for performing a specific role.

Every agent:

- Has a defined responsibility
- Owns no global state
- Produces explainable decisions
- Communicates through structured messages

Examples:

- Calendar Agent
- Email Agent
- Research Agent

---

## AI Layer

One of the five hierarchical intelligence levels of Life OS.

Each layer has different authority, responsibilities and decision scope.

Layers:

1. Vision & Governance
2. Strategic Intelligence
3. Operational Management
4. Execution Engine
5. Autonomous Micro Agents

---

## AI Provider

A service capable of executing language or reasoning models.

Examples include:

- OpenAI
- Google Gemini
- Anthropic Claude
- Local LLMs

The system must remain provider-independent.

---

# B

## Board

The highest decision-making entity inside Life OS.

The Board consists of Layer 1 agents.

Responsibilities include:

- Vision
- Mission
- Ethics
- Long-term strategy

---

## Briefing

A structured executive summary generated by the system.

Examples:

- Morning Brief
- Evening Review
- Weekly Executive Report
- Monthly Board Report

---

# C

## Capability

A feature or behaviour provided by an agent or module.

Examples:

- Schedule optimisation
- Knowledge retrieval
- Forecast generation

---

## Context

The collection of information available when making a decision.

Context may include:

- Current projects
- Calendar
- Energy
- Goals
- Recent conversations
- Historical behaviour

---

## Context Window

The subset of memory actively supplied to an AI model during reasoning.

Context should contain only information relevant to the current task.

---

# D

## Decision

A recommendation or selection made by an AI layer or a user.

Every decision should include:

- Rationale
- Alternatives
- Expected impact
- Confidence
- Risks

---

## Decision Engine

The subsystem responsible for evaluating competing alternatives.

Evaluation considers:

- Strategic alignment
- Risk
- ROI
- Dependencies
- Energy
- Time
- Probability of success

---

## Dependency

A relationship where one item cannot begin until another is completed.

Dependencies may exist between:

- Goals
- Projects
- Tasks
- Agents
- Workflows

---

## Director

A Layer 3 AI responsible for one life domain.

Examples:

- Career Director
- Health Director
- Finance Director

Directors coordinate projects within their domain.

---

## Domain

A major area of life managed independently while remaining interconnected.

Examples:

- Career
- Research
- Health
- Finance
- Family

---

# E

## Energy Score

A numerical estimate of the user's available physical and mental capacity.

Used for:

- Scheduling
- Prioritisation
- Recommendation generation

---

## Event

A significant occurrence within the system.

Examples:

- Task completed
- Meeting scheduled
- Goal achieved
- New research paper imported

Events are published to the Event Bus.

---

## Event Bus

The communication backbone of Life OS.

Allows independent modules and agents to exchange information without direct coupling.

---

## Executive Dashboard

The primary interface providing strategic visibility over the user's life.

Displays:

- Goals
- KPIs
- Risks
- Priorities
- Forecasts
- Health metrics

---

# F

## Forecast

A prediction generated using available information.

Forecasts may estimate:

- Success probability
- Time requirements
- Financial outcomes
- Health trends

---

# G

## Goal

A desired future outcome.

Goals are measurable.

Goals have:

- Owner
- Priority
- Deadline
- Success metrics

Goals contain one or more Projects.

---

## Governance

The policies and principles that guide strategic decisions.

Governance protects long-term objectives from short-term optimisation.

---

# H

## Habit

A recurring behaviour intended to produce long-term benefits.

Habits are scheduled and monitored by the Execution Layer.

Examples:

- Exercise
- Reading
- Prayer
- Meditation

---

## Human-in-the-Loop

A design principle requiring human approval for strategic or high-impact decisions.

Life OS assists rather than replaces human judgement.

---

# I

## Identity Memory

Persistent information describing who the user is.

Examples:

- Personal profile
- Skills
- Preferences
- Languages
- Values

---

## Initiative

A collection of related projects working toward a strategic objective.

---

# K

## Key Performance Indicator (KPI)

A measurable indicator used to evaluate progress.

Examples:

- Papers published
- Weekly exercise sessions
- Monthly savings
- Deep work hours

---

## Knowledge Graph

A semantic network connecting entities, concepts, documents and relationships.

Supports advanced reasoning and discovery.

---

## Knowledge Item

Any piece of stored information.

Examples:

- Note
- Book
- Article
- PDF
- Video
- Voice memo

---

# L

## Layer

A level within the hierarchical intelligence architecture.

Higher layers define strategy.

Lower layers perform execution.

---

## Life Domain

A broad category representing a significant area of life.

Domains include:

- Career
- Health
- Finance
- Research
- Learning
- Family
- Spirituality

---

## Life Score

A composite indicator representing overall long-term wellbeing.

Calculated using multiple weighted dimensions.

---

# M

## Memory

Persistent knowledge retained across sessions.

Memory types include:

- Identity
- Goals
- Projects
- Health
- Relationships
- Finance
- Research
- Decisions
- Failures
- Successes

---

## Memory Engine

The subsystem responsible for storing, retrieving and linking memories.

Supports semantic search and long-term context.

---

## Micro-Agent

A specialised Layer 5 agent responsible for exactly one operational responsibility.

Examples:

- Email Agent
- Workout Agent
- Backup Agent

---

## Milestone

A significant checkpoint within a project.

Milestones divide projects into measurable stages.

---

## Mission

The fundamental purpose guiding all long-term activities.

Mission is defined by Layer 1.

---

# O

## Objective

A measurable target contributing toward strategic goals.

Objectives should be specific and time-bound.

---

## Opportunity

A potential improvement identified by strategic analysis.

Every opportunity should include:

- Expected benefit
- Cost
- Risk
- Confidence

---

# P

## Priority

A ranking indicating execution importance.

Priority considers:

- Urgency
- Importance
- Strategic alignment
- Dependencies

---

## Project

A coordinated collection of tasks intended to achieve a defined outcome.

Projects belong to a Goal.

Projects contain Milestones and Tasks.

---

## Project Director

The AI responsible for coordinating a specific project.

---

# R

## Recommendation

An action proposed by the system.

Recommendations always include:

- Explanation
- Expected benefit
- Risks
- Confidence level

---

## Relationship Graph

A semantic representation of relationships between users, people, organisations and projects.

---

## Resource

Anything required to complete work.

Examples:

- Time
- Money
- Knowledge
- Equipment
- Attention

---

## Risk

A potential event capable of negatively affecting objectives.

Every risk includes:

- Probability
- Impact
- Severity
- Mitigation
- Owner

---

## Roadmap

A long-term sequence of planned objectives and milestones.

Roadmaps exist at:

- Annual
- Quarterly
- Project
- Strategic levels

---

# S

## Second Brain

The integrated knowledge management system of Life OS.

Stores and organises all external knowledge.

---

## Semantic Memory

Memory organised according to meaning rather than chronological order.

Supports intelligent retrieval and reasoning.

---

## Strategic Alignment

A measure of how strongly an action supports long-term objectives.

---

## Strategic Objective

A high-level outcome defined by the Vision Layer.

Strategic objectives guide quarterly and annual planning.

---

## Success Metric

A measurable indicator used to evaluate whether an objective has been achieved.

---

## System Health

An aggregate assessment of the operational status of Life OS.

Includes:

- Service availability
- Agent performance
- Data integrity
- AI responsiveness

---

# T

## Task

The smallest executable unit of work.

Tasks should be:

- Atomic
- Measurable
- Time-estimated
- Assigned
- Prioritised

Tasks may generate Events.

---

## Timeline

A chronological representation of plans, milestones and events.

---

# U

## User Profile

The structured representation of the user's identity, preferences, goals and constraints.

---

# V

## Value

A guiding principle influencing decision-making.

Values are defined by Layer 1.

---

## Vector Store

A database used for storing semantic embeddings.

Supports retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and semantic search.

---

## Vision

The desired long-term future state for the user's life.

Vision is the highest-level planning construct within Life OS.

All strategies, projects and tasks ultimately derive from the Vision.

---

# W

## Workflow

A repeatable sequence of actions performed by one or more agents.

Workflows may include:

- Human approval
- AI reasoning
- External integrations
- Automation
- Notifications

---

# Living Document

This glossary is a living document.

New terminology should be added whenever new architectural concepts, AI capabilities or system components are introduced.

Definitions should remain concise, consistent and technology-agnostic wherever possible.
